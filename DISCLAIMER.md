# Disclaimer

This project is an **educational reverse-engineering devlog**. Its goal is to
document *how* the text system of *Rudra no Hihō / Treasure of the Rudras*
(SNES, Square, 1996) is structured — as a study of retro text engines and
encoding/compression techniques.

## What this repository does NOT contain

- ❌ **No ROM image** (or any portion of one). You must obtain your own legal
  copy to run the tools.
- ❌ **No game graphics, fonts, tilesets, or audio** extracted from the game.
- ❌ **No bulk extracted script / translated dialogue.** The game's text is
  copyrighted by Square. Short fragments may appear *only* as fair-use
  illustrations of a technique.

## What it DOES contain

- ✅ Original tooling (our own code).
- ✅ Notes describing the *format and structure* of the encoding (offsets,
  byte meanings, control codes) — i.e. methodology, not the copyrighted work.
- ✅ An encoding/`.tbl` table, in line with long-standing romhacking community
  practice of sharing cipher-key tables.
